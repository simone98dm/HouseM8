# 01 — Marketing

Scope: `app/pages/index.vue`, `app/pages/pricing.vue`,
`app/components/landing/{LandingHero,LandingFeatures,LandingHowItWorks,LandingPricing,LandingFaq}.vue`.
Shell (`page/Header`, `page/Footer`) is in group 00.

Scores: A11y 3/4 · Perf 3/4 · Theming 2/4 · Responsive 3/4 · Anti-patterns 0/4 = **11/20 (Acceptable)**

## Summary

Technically sound: semantic sections, one `h1`, native `<details>` FAQ, lazy
section chunks, all copy through i18n. The problem is the design itself. The
landing page is the exact template `PRODUCT.md` names as an anti-reference:
gradient hero with a sparkle pill and gradient-text headline, a fake browser
window with a glow behind it, three emoji cards, a grid of identical icon
cards, an eyebrow, and a gradient CTA band with a dot grid. Seven distinct
tells. Separately, the two pricing surfaces disagree on plans and prices, and
prices are hardcoded in templates.

## Working well — keep

- Section landmarks with ids; single `h1`; `<details>/<summary>` FAQ with no JS.
- `LazyLanding*` code-splitting; hydration-safe auth CTA keyed on `hydrated`.
- All landing copy in i18n; CTA focus ring defined on the gradient band.
