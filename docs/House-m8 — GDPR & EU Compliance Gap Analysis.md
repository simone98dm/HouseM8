# House-m8 — GDPR & EU Compliance Gap Analysis

Oct 26, 2026 · @Feudo Team

## Executive summary

**Verdict: not yet launch-ready for the EU/IT market.** 8 P0 blockers remain, plus 11 P1 and 8 P2 gaps. The groundwork is better than average: there are no trackers, assets are self-hosted, there is a bilingual Art. 13 policy, a 30-day soft-delete with pseudonymization, a self-service export, hashed tokens and security headers. The blockers sit in four places: an over-collected profile, consent the policy promises but the app never captures, an incomplete erasure, and paid subscriptions without the matching legal and consumer-law layer.

Top 5 must-fix items before launch:

1. **Stop over-collecting.** Drop birthplace, nationality, birthdate and smoking habit. Smoking is recorded as `false` for every user by default (B1, A2).
2. **Capture the consent the policy promises** for optional profile data, with withdrawal in Settings (A1, A3).
3. **Make erasure real.** Cancel the Stripe subscription, delete the user's notes and reports, end memberships, hand over house ownership (C1, C2).
4. **Disclose Stripe and publish real Terms** with provider identity, B2C clauses, renewal and the withdrawal right (D1, D2, D3).
5. **Make checkout lawful.** Collect the withdrawal waiver and ToS acceptance, show VAT-inclusive prices, and invoice through SDI (E1).

Outside the code: sign and archive the DPAs, write the Art. 30 record, set up the business (P.IVA), and prepare a breach procedure.

## Scope, method and legal framework

The audit covers the whole `house-m8-fe` repository on branch `feature/transfer-house-ownership` as of 26 Oct 2026: Nuxt app (`app/`), Nitro API (`server/`), DB migrations (`supabase/migrations/`, `database/`), emails, cron jobs, PWA and public legal pages. It is a static code review against the project's `gdpr-guidelines` skill; nothing was tested against production infrastructure or contracts.

| Framework                                                           | What it requires of House-m8                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| GDPR (Reg. EU 2016/679)                                             | Lawful basis per purpose, transparency (Arts. 12-14), data subject rights (Arts. 15-22), privacy by design (Art. 25), processors (Art. 28), records (Art. 30), security and breach handling (Arts. 32-34), transfers (Chapter V) |
| Codice Privacy (D.Lgs. 196/2003 as amended by D.Lgs. 101/2018)      | Digital age of consent 14 (Art. 2-quinquies); Garante powers and sanctions                                                                                                                                                       |
| Garante cookie guidelines (Provv. 231 of 10 Jun 2021)               | Prior blocking, banner with X = reject, equal Accept/Reject, consent proof, no re-prompt within 6 months                                                                                                                         |
| ePrivacy Directive 2002/58 (Art. 5(3))                              | Consent before storing/reading non-essential data on the device (cookies, localStorage, service worker caches used for tracking)                                                                                                 |
| Codice del Consumo (D.Lgs. 206/2005) + Consumer Rights Dir. 2011/83 | Pre-contract info and 14-day withdrawal for paid subscriptions, express waiver for immediate digital service, "withdraw" button (Dir. 2023/2673, applies from 19 Jun 2026)                                                       |
| D.Lgs. 70/2003 (e-commerce)                                         | Provider identity: company name, address, P.IVA, PEC/contact on the site                                                                                                                                                         |
| European Accessibility Act (D.Lgs. 82/2022)                         | E-commerce services to consumers accessible from 28 Jun 2025; accessibility statement                                                                                                                                            |
| NIS2 / DSA                                                          | Likely out of scope (micro-enterprise, no public content sharing); confirm                                                                                                                                                       |

Severity scale used below: **P0** blocks launch (unlawful processing or a missing mandatory right), **P1** must be fixed shortly after launch, **P2** good practice / risk reduction.

## Organizational requirements (outside the code)

These 8 items are paperwork and process. A Garante inspection asks for them first, and none exist in the repo today.

- [ ] **Record of processing activities (Art. 30).** The <250-employee exemption does not apply, because processing is not occasional and includes Art. 9 data. Build it from the inventory above (purposes, categories, recipients, transfers, retention, security measures).
- [ ] **DPAs signed and archived** with Vercel, Supabase, Resend and Stripe. Keep a copy of each SCC module and a transfer impact assessment for Supabase (Singapore) and Resend (US).
- [ ] **Legitimate interest assessment** for security logging and the rate limiter (F2, F3).
- [ ] **DPIA screening.** Not mandatory if B1/B4 are applied (no evaluation of individuals, no special-category data). If smoking habit or ratings stay, run a DPIA (Garante list, Provv. 467/2018).
- [ ] **Data breach procedure** (Arts. 33-34). Who decides, how to scope affected users from `security_events`, the 72-hour notice template for the Garante's online form, an internal breach register, and a user-notification email template in IT and EN.
- [ ] **Rights-request procedure.** Identity check, one-month deadline, register entry in `gdpr_requests`, and replies from the dedicated mailbox.
- [ ] **DPO evaluation.** Not required (no large-scale systematic monitoring), but record the reasoning.
- [ ] **Business setup for paid plans.** P.IVA, VAT/OSS registration, e-invoicing through SDI, an Accessibility Statement (EAA, for the checkout flow), and a lawyer review of Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy and Terms in Italian and English.
